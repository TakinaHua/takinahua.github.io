# Design

The portfolio reads like an engineering publication set against a waterfront at dusk. The opening spread pairs Barry’s own photograph with his introduction. Experience follows immediately for recruiters, then projects and the personal and technical background.

## Foundations

- Charcoal is the shared page ground. Navy surfaces and two diffuse lighting fields provide depth. Desaturated periwinkle marks actions, current location, and keyboard focus.
- Baskervville sets the name and section titles. The interface and prose use the system sans. Dates use tabular numerals, with short measures and generous line spacing in running copy.
- A centered, fluid 1120px grid sets the outer edges. Hairlines carry the rhythm between sections. Photography and interactive projects can occupy solid surfaces, while education and experience use rules and typography.
- Keep the waterfront crop recognizable. Use cool tonal grading, a low-contrast texture, and restrained photograph registration marks. Use existing photographs and project previews.

## Interaction

- Experience is the first hero action. The header links to the main sections. The section compass retains the complete index and its desktop wheel behavior. Compact screens use a labeled disclosure.
- Project links and interest buttons have visible focus and distinct hover and pressed states. An active interest highlights its associated skills and preserves the related project link.
- The Projects rule tracks entry into the viewport. Reading content is visible at rest. Reduced motion disables the scroll-driven effect, smooth scrolling, and spatial feedback.
- Images and multi-column regions stack on narrow screens. The navigation disclosure and controls remain readable and touch-sized.
