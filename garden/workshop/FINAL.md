# Painted workshop animations

## Sakura and petals

Draw `animations/petals/petals-sheet.png` at **(1140, 0)** over the 1672 × 941 workshop background. Each frame is **532 × 520**. Read the 48 frames left to right across eight columns and six rows, advancing every 100 ms for a 4.8-second loop. Placement and timing are also in `animations/petals/petals.json`.

The flowering canopy sways in a breeze while eleven pink petals turn and descend behind the window bars. Frame zero is transparent. Open `animations/petals/petals-in-scene.apng` for the half-size review.

## Tea steam

Draw `animations/steam/steam-sheet.png` at **(350, 540)**. Each frame is **130 × 140**. Read the 48 frames left to right across eight columns and six rows, advancing every 100 ms for a 4.8-second loop. Placement and timing are also in `animations/steam/steam.json`.

Composite the steam over the lamp using the sheet’s alpha channel. Three cream-white wisps rise at staggered intervals and fade at the edges, reaching 45% opacity at their densest. Frame zero is transparent. Open `animations/steam/steam-in-scene.apng` to see the steam over the animated lamp at half size.
